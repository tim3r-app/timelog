Privacy policy

Time Log: Tracker & Notes stores your intervals, notes, settings and drafts on this device. We do not collect or transmit them. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

Reports and backups leave the app only when you choose to share or export them. The selected app or storage provider handles that file under its own policies. Backups are not encrypted and may include deleted records retained for safe restore merging.

You can delete intervals in the app. To permanently remove all local data, clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall Time Log: Tracker & Notes. Delete exported copies separately from their saved locations. Automatic Android app backup is disabled.

Support: tim3r.app@gmail.com. If you email us, your email address and message are used to answer your request. Do not attach private logs unless needed.

Effective date: 23 September 2026.
